About The Codex

The Codex is an independent learning project designed to encourage clear thinking, intellectual curiosity, self-understanding and responsible reasoning.

Book I — Knowledge

Explore what knowledge means, how evidence supports claims, how reasoning works and why human understanding has limits.

Book II — The Mind

Explore consciousness, thoughts, emotions, memory, behaviour, habits, motivation, identity and the process of understanding yourself.

Created by BNG Digital Studio

BNG Digital Studio is the development studio behind The Codex, creating accessible, mobile-friendly digital learning experiences.

Learning philosophy

Examine evidence, test reasoning, recognise uncertainty and remain willing to revise your beliefs.

The Codex is an educational resource. It does not replace formal academic instruction or professional mental health advice.
